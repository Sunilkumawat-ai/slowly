      03
      About
      
      
      
        
      How it works
      
        
      Your browser downloads a compressed open-weight model once and stores it. After that, the model runs on your phone's GPU through WebGPU. Long chats are read in parts and combined.
      
        
      Why open weights
      
        
      The weights are public, so the model runs here with no account, no API key and no per-use fee. Your chat is never sent to a server, and you can switch models.
      
        
      Limits
      
        
      Small models can miss details or misread tone. Treat the summary as a quick catch-up and check the original chat before acting on anything important. Needs a recent Chrome on Android with WebGPU.
      
      
      
      
      Built with open-weight models for the Hacktoberfest 2026 Open-Source AI Challenge.
